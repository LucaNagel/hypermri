Research-use notice
===================

HyperMRI is research software. It has not been validated or approved as a
medical device and is not intended for clinical diagnosis, treatment
decisions, or other clinical use. Its outputs may be incomplete, incorrect, or
unsuitable for a particular experiment.

Scientific validation
---------------------

Users are responsible for independently validating the methods and results for
their data, acquisition protocol, scanner configuration, and scientific
question. Example workflows, reference values, and passing software tests do
not establish scientific validity for a new experiment.

Privacy and compliance
----------------------

Users are responsible for complying with applicable ethical, privacy,
institutional, and regulatory requirements.

Do not publish patient data, identifying metadata, credentials, or confidential
research data in GitHub issues, discussions, pull requests, or examples. Prefer
synthetic or fully anonymized minimal examples. Suspected security
vulnerabilities must be reported privately using the contact information in
the repository's ``SECURITY.md`` file.

Support boundary
----------------

Community support is provided on a best-effort basis and is not a substitute
for independent scientific validation, professional medical judgment, or
scanner-vendor support.
